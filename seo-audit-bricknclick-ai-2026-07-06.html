Executive Summary

Bricknclick is in the top tier of technical search readiness for a site its size. The foundations are genuinely strong: valid, layered schema on every page (Organization, BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Service), a complete sameAs entity graph, a real hand-authored llms.txt (a signal fewer than 1% of sites ship), prerendered crawlable HTML, HSTS, and 100% image-alt coverage. The most urgent fix is anonymous authorship — bylines read "by IIM alumni" and the author/founder schema names no human, which caps E-E-A-T for both Google and AI engines. The biggest untapped opportunity is AEO schema: FAQPage markup lives only on /services, while your step-by-step blog posts are ideal candidates for FAQPage + HowTo markup to win featured snippets and AI citations.

What This Audit Could Not Measure

This audit is based on server-rendered HTML. The following need dedicated tools:

  • Core Web Vitals / real page speed — run pagespeed.web.dev (Google PageSpeed Insights) per template.
  • Mobile rendering & interaction — verify in a real device / Lighthouse mobile.
  • Backlink profile & domain authority — Ahrefs / Semrush.
  • Actual AI-citation share — track brand mentions inside Perplexity / ChatGPT Search / AI Overviews over time.

Glossary

TermPlain-English definition
SEOSearch Engine Optimization — getting ranked in traditional results (Google, Bing) via titles, meta tags, headings, links, and schema.
GEOGenerative Engine Optimization — being authoritative and fact-rich enough that AI engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews) cite you inside their generated answers.
AEOAnswer Engine Optimization — structuring content so engines can lift a direct answer into featured snippets, People Also Ask, and voice results.
